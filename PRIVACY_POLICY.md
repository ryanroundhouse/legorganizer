# Privacy Policy for Legorganizer

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026

Legorganizer is a LEGO part and storage-bin organizer. Your collection data
stays on your device. The only time anything leaves your device is when you
choose to identify a part from a photo, or when the app loads a part
thumbnail image.

## Summary

- Legorganizer does **not** require an account.
- Legorganizer does **not** collect personal information, usage analytics,
  advertising identifiers, or location.
- Legorganizer does **not** use advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or
  third-party analytics.
- Legorganizer **does** send a photo to a third-party service when you ask it
  to identify a part from the camera or your photo library.
- Legorganizer **does** download part thumbnail images from a third-party
  catalog service.

## Data storage

All part and bin information you enter is stored locally on your device in
app-local storage. It stays there unless you export it yourself. Uninstalling
the app deletes it.

## Camera and photo library

The camera and photo library are used only when you tap the "identify from
photo" action. The photo you take or choose is uploaded to Brickognize
(api.brickognize.com) so it can return the matching LEGO part number. The
photo is not stored by Legorganizer and is not linked to you. Brickognize's
handling of uploaded images is governed by its own privacy policy at
<https://brickognize.com/>.

## Part images

Part thumbnails are loaded from Rebrickable's content delivery network
(cdn.rebrickable.com). These requests contain the part number being
displayed and the usual technical information any web request carries, such
as your IP address. Legorganizer sends no account or identity information
with these requests.

## Data shared with third parties

| Recipient | What is sent | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brickognize | A photo of a LEGO part | Only when you choose to identify a part from a photo | Part identification |
| Rebrickable | Part numbers | When a part thumbnail is shown | Displaying part images |

Nothing else is shared with anyone.

## Apple App Store (App Privacy) declaration

- **Data Used to Track You:** None
- **Data Linked to You:** None
- **Data Not Linked to You:** Photos or Videos (used for App Functionality
  only, when you identify a part from a photo)

## Google Play (Data Safety) declaration

- **Data shared:** Photos (only when you identify a part from a photo, for
  app functionality)
- **Data collected:** None stored by the developer
- **Data is processed on device** except for the two network uses described
  above

## Children's privacy

Legorganizer does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If app behavior changes in a future release, this policy will be updated
before that release ships.
